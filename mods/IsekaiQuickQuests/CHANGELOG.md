# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — Quick Quests, a standalone shortcut — 2026-09-17

### Added
- **Accept with a middle-click.** Accept an eligible ISEKAI quest straight from its letter when no pawn, reward or option needs to be chosen.
- **Keep the normal quest flow when a choice is needed.** Those quests open the usual quest tab instead of being accepted automatically.
- **A separate on/off setting.** The shortcut starts enabled. English and Russian interface text is included.

### Using both add-ons
- Update Balanced Stats to 1.2.0 or later before enabling both mods. Quick Quests also works on its own.
- A disabled quick-accept setting from the old combined mod is not imported; turn the shortcut off in Quick Quests settings if preferred.
