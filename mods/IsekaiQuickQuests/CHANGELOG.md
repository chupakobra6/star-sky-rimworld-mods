# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-17

- Released middle-click ISEKAI quest acceptance as a standalone mod, separated from Balanced Stats & Quick Quests.
- Accepts available quests only when no pawn, reward or option choice is required. Other ISEKAI quests open in the normal quest tab.
- Added an independent on/off setting, enabled by default; the old combined mod’s disabled quick-accept setting is not imported.
- Requires a separate subscription. Update Balanced Stats to 1.2.0 or later before using both mods; Quick Quests also works on its own.
- Included English and Russian interface text. This mod does not change stat allocation or store colony save data.
