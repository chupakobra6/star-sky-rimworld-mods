# Gas Wall Torches [Star Sky Mods]

Wall-mounted torches powered by your Helixien gas network. No wood refills

Choose from four flame types. Mount them on a wall, connect them to gas and use the manual switch, or let LightsOut turn them on and off

Made at the request of svg
<!-- rimmods:readme intro end -->

## What it does

<!-- rimmods:readme features begin -->
- **Warm flame** – warm light, with no DLC required
- **Bloodflame** – red light and a +2 mood bonus for nearby pawns with an active bloodfeeder gene
- **Toxflame** – green light and a +2 mood bonus for nearby pawns with an active pollution stimulus gene. Does not produce pollution
- **Darkflame** – darklight instead of ordinary light

Each torch has a light radius of 10 tiles and consumes 0.02 Helixien gas per game tick while burning. The bloodflame and toxflame bonuses apply within 7 tiles of a burning torch of the matching type

Switching a torch off stops its flame, light, heat, sound and gas use. It also goes out when its gas supply runs dry
<!-- rimmods:readme features end -->

## Controls and use

<!-- rimmods:readme how-to-use begin -->
Research Gas Extraction from Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded, then find the torches under **Furniture**. Each costs 15 steel. Place one against a wall in any of four directions and connect its tile to a Helixien gas pipe

Use the torch's switch to turn it on or off

With [LightsOut](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2584269293) installed, the torch follows that mod's lighting rules for the room it faces. Automatic shutoff extinguishes the flame and stops the light, heat, sound and gas use

LightsOut turns the torch back on when its room rules allow it, provided the torch has gas and its manual switch is on. Switching it off manually keeps it off. Use LightsOut's **Keep On** control to exclude a torch from automatic shutoff
<!-- rimmods:readme how-to-use end -->

## Requirements and installation

<!-- rimmods:readme compatibility begin -->
Requires **RimWorld 1.6**, [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077), [Vanilla Expanded Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2023507013) and [Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2877699803). Load this mod after its dependencies

Bloodflame and toxflame require **Biotech**; darkflame requires **Ideology**. The warm torch is available without either DLC. Includes English and Russian text

LightsOut is optional. If installed, load it before Gas Wall Torches. It can automatically control all four torches added by this mod
<!-- rimmods:readme compatibility end -->
