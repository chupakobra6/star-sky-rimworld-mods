Light your rooms with wall-mounted flames supplied by your Helixien gas network, without hauling wood to each torch

Gas Wall Torches adds four distinct flames for your colony. Build them against a wall, connect them to gas and switch them manually, or let LightsOut manage them automatically

Made at the request of svg
<!-- rimmods:readme intro end -->

## What it does

<!-- rimmods:readme features begin -->
- **Warm flame** – a familiar torch with warm light
- **Bloodflame** – red light that gives a nearby pawn with the active bloodfeeder gene a +2 mood thought while the torch burns
- **Toxflame** – green light that gives a nearby pawn with the active pollution stimulus gene a +2 mood thought while the torch burns, without releasing pollution
- **Darkflame** – true darklight for a different kind of room

Each torch has a light radius of 10 tiles and consumes 0.02 Helixien gas per game tick while burning. Bloodflame and toxflame mood effects reach 7 tiles from the corresponding burning torch

The flame, light, heat and sound follow the same burning state. When a torch is switched off or has no gas, it stops burning and consuming gas
<!-- rimmods:readme features end -->

## Controls and use

<!-- rimmods:readme how-to-use begin -->
Research Gas Extraction from Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded, then find the torches under **Furniture**. Each costs 15 steel. Place one against a wall in any of four directions and connect its tile to a Helixien gas pipe

Use the torch's normal switch to turn it on or off. Without LightsOut, this is the way to control it

With [LightsOut](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2584269293) installed, the torch follows its room lighting rules on the illuminated side of the wall. Automatic shutdown also stops the flame, heat, sound and gas use; the torch lights again when LightsOut calls for it, gas is available and its manual switch is on. The manual off switch takes priority. Use LightsOut's **Keep On** control if you want that torch excluded from automatic shutdown
<!-- rimmods:readme how-to-use end -->

## Requirements and installation

<!-- rimmods:readme compatibility begin -->
Requires **RimWorld 1.6**, [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2009463077), [Vanilla Expanded Framework](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2023507013) and [Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2877699803). Load this mod after its dependencies

Bloodflame and toxflame require **Biotech**; darkflame requires **Ideology**. The warm torch is available without either DLC. English and Russian text is included

LightsOut is optional. If installed, load it before Gas Wall Torches. The integration controls the torches added by this mod
<!-- rimmods:readme compatibility end -->
