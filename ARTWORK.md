# Artwork sources

The Star Sky Mods mark preserves the geometry of the original Starboard logo. The white-on-black version inverts its original colours. Star Sky Mods’ cosmic backgrounds, rounded frames, version ribbons, section headers and typography are composed with vector tools; no ImageGen imagery is used.

The three ISEKAI covers use frame 0 of the **800 × 450** [original artwork](https://images.steamusercontent.com/ugc/12230229214356412021/59AE4D6DDC7C4D2DF10F81734F88B4BB70BF7631/) from [ISEKAI RPG LEVELING](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3657580708), by **POUPUN**. These are the original mod’s artwork with our titles and frame, not gameplay screenshots demonstrating the add-ons.

The unpublished **RimSaves Russian-translation cover example** uses the original preview from [RimSaves](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1713367505), by **aRandomKiwi**. It preserves the RimSaves wordmark and adds a Russian label, flag, logo and frame. The source is 1047 × 595; the example is 800 × 450 without upscaling. This preview does not mean that a RimSaves translation is included in this repository.

Typography is rendered from **Oswald**, distributed under the [SIL Open Font License](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Oswald/license). The font software is not included here; the published assets are rendered PNG images.
